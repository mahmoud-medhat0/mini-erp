### English Technical Overview

This policy decision pack establishes the accounting and architectural choices required before implementing **Phase 6: Fixed Assets & Depreciation Engine** in Mini ERP.

Fixed Asset Management tracks long-term capitalized assets (e.g., equipment, vehicles, machinery, computers, furniture), computes periodic depreciation expenses, maintains net book values ($\text{NBV}$), and processes asset disposals, sales, or scrapping.

This document presents 12 core policy choices, recommends the safest production defaults for Mini ERP, details the required General Ledger (GL) account mappings, provides a formal owner decision checklist, and outlines the slice-by-slice implementation roadmap for Phase 6.

## 2. Plain-Language Explanation of Fixed Asset Accounting

In double-entry financial accounting:

1. **Capitalization vs. Expensing**:
   - Items purchased for short-term operational consumption are expensed immediately (e.g., office stationery).
   - Long-term assets with a useful life exceeding 12 months are **capitalized** as Assets on the Balance Sheet rather than expensed all at once.

2. **Depreciation**:
   - Depreciation spreads the capitalized cost of a fixed asset over its estimated useful life to match expenses with the revenues generated by the asset (Matching Principle).
   - Periodic Journal Entry:
     $$\text{Debit: Depreciation Expense (Income Statement)} \quad / \quad \text{Credit: Accumulated Depreciation (Balance Sheet Contra-Asset)}$$

3. **Net Book Value ($\text{NBV}$)**:
   $$\text{Net Book Value} = \text{Historical Cost} - \text{Accumulated Depreciation}$$

4. **Asset Disposal / Retirement**:
   - When an asset is sold, retired, or scrapped:
     - Remove the Historical Cost from Assets ($\text{Credit}$).
     - Remove the Accumulated Depreciation ($\text{Debit}$).
     - Record proceeds received ($\text{Debit Cash/Bank}$).
     - Recognize Gain or Loss on Disposal ($\text{Income Statement}$).

## 4. Deep-Dive Comparison of Technical Options

### 1. Depreciation Method Comparison

- **Straight-Line Method (RECOMMENDED)**:
  $$\text{Monthly Depreciation} = \text{intdiv}\left(\frac{\text{Historical Cost} - \text{Salvage Value}}{\text{Useful Life in Months}}\right)$$
  - **Pros**: Perfectly deterministic, integer minor-unit exact ($\text{EGP Minor}$), standard across IFRS/GAAP for general ERPs.
  - **Cons**: Does not accelerate expense for fast-depreciating tech assets.

- **Declining Balance / Double Declining Balance**:
  - Requires percentage rates and salvage threshold locks.
  - **Cons**: High complexity, rounding drift risk in minor units.

- **Recommendation**: **Straight-Line Method Only** for Phase 6.

### 2. General Ledger (GL) Mapping Architecture

Each Fixed Asset Category (`product_category` or dedicated `fixed_asset_category`) requires the following 5 GL Account Mappings:

1. **Asset Cost Account (`asset_cost_account_id`)**: Balance Sheet Asset (e.g., `1510 - Equipment Cost`).
2. **Accumulated Depreciation Account (`accumulated_depreciation_account_id`)**: Balance Sheet Contra-Asset (e.g., `1519 - Accum Dep Equipment`).
3. **Depreciation Expense Account (`depreciation_expense_account_id`)**: Income Statement Operating Expense (e.g., `5210 - Depreciation Expense Equipment`).
4. **Gain on Disposal Account (`gain_on_disposal_account_id`)**: Income Statement Other Income (e.g., `4910 - Gain on Asset Disposal`).
5. **Loss on Disposal Account (`loss_on_disposal_account_id`)**: Income Statement Other Expense (e.g., `5910 - Loss on Asset Disposal`).
6. **Fixed Asset Clearing Account (`asset_clearing_account_id`) [Optional]**: Balance Sheet Asset/Clearing (e.g., `1599 - Fixed Asset Clearing`).

### 3. Accounting Entry Flow Models

#### Capitalization Posting (New Asset)
$$\begin{array}{lll}
\text{Dr} & \text{Asset Cost Account} & \$100,000 \\
\text{Cr} & \text{Fixed Asset Clearing Account} & \quad \$100,000
\end{array}$$

#### Monthly Depreciation Posting (Run Engine)
$$\begin{array}{lll}
\text{Dr} & \text{Depreciation Expense Account} & \$2,000 \\
\text{Cr} & \text{Accumulated Depreciation Account} & \quad \$2,000
\end{array}$$

#### Disposal Posting (Gain Example: Cost = $100k, Accum Dep = $60k, Proceeds = $50k)
$$\begin{array}{lll}
\text{Dr} & \text{Cash / Bank Account} & \$50,000 \\
\text{Dr} & \text{Accumulated Depreciation Account} & \$60,000 \\
\text{Cr} & \text{Asset Cost Account} & \quad \$100,000 \\
\text{Cr} & \text{Gain on Asset Disposal} & \quad \$10,000
\end{array}$$
